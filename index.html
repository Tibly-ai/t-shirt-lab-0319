LAB STANDARDS

Our printing methods match the strict requirements of major retail brands. Zero shortcuts on cure times or screen density.

01

Plastisol Solid Deposition

Ultra-opaque prints that pop on dark garments. High pigment loading combined with rigid mesh tension yields clean vector lines and maximum ink density.

TENSION: 25-30 N/CM OPTIMIZED
02

Water-Based Discharge

Bleaches the fabric's original dye and replaces it with ink, resulting in an incredibly soft hand feel that becomes part of the shirt itself after one wash.

HAND FEEL: ZERO INK OPTIMIZED
03

Precision Color Match

We formulate custom inks under controlled lighting conditions. Every color code is recorded for subsequent reprints to guarantee identical consistency.
